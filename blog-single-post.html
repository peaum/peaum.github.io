UNDERSTANDING THE 16-BIT ADC ON ADUCM410

The ADUCM410 ADC can deliver excellent performance when reference quality, acquisition windows, and conversion timing are tuned for your source impedance. In this write-up, I walk through the register sequence and practical initialization path I use for stable startup behavior.

A key lesson is that precision depends on firmware discipline as much as silicon capability: schedule conversions away from high-noise switching events, average intelligently, and maintain calibration constants in nonvolatile storage.

I also compare interrupt-driven and DMA-driven collection paths and show where each model wins depending on bandwidth, latency, and CPU budget constraints in a Cortex-M33 system.
